?bABOUT?n

     This was inspired by the Library of Babel, an infinite library with every
     book that existed in the past, exists now and will exist in the future.
     This is a digital version of it called the Pager of Babel. You can page
     through infinite logs with entries of 999 pages per entry. Just like the
     library, this contains every page that could be printed, is printed and
     will be printed.

?bCONTROLS?n

     ?bb?n, ?bw?n    Previous page

     ?be?n, ?bj?n, ?bdown arrow?n,
             Down one line

     ?bf?n       Search for phrase

     ?bh?n       Show help manual

     ?bk?n, ?by?n, ?bup arrow?n
             Up one line

     ?bq?n       Quit

     ?bspace?n, ?bz?n
             Next page

     ?b:?n       Enter command

?bCOMMANDS?n

     ?bentry?n ?uentry?n
             Go to entry

     ?bf?n ?uphrase?n, ?bsearch?n ?uphrase?n
             Search for phrase

     ?bh?n, ?bhelp?n
             Display this help screen

     ?blog?n ?ulog?n
             Go to log

     ?bq?n, ?bquit?n
             Quit

     ?b<any number>?n
             Jump to page
